# ApnaWallet User Manual

## Registration and Login

Register with a name, email and strong password. A Personal account is created automatically. Use Sign In for later sessions.

## Transactions

- Add Cash increases Cash or Bank.
- Expense decreases the selected balance.
- Transfer moves funds between balances without changing total available funds.

## Lending and Credit

Lending records money owed to the user. Repayments reduce outstanding lending and restore cash. Credit records money owed by the user and repayments reduce liability without being ordinary expenses.

## Savings and Investments

Savings deposits transfer spendable funds into a goal. Investment purchases are recorded against cost basis; sales update realized gain/loss.

## Budgets and Recurring Expenses

Monthly budgets compare expense activity against configured limits. Recurring expenses are recorded only for eligible scheduled occurrences.

## Notifications and Reports

Notifications cover daily expenses, credit due dates and recurring expenses. Reports support period, account and category filters, CSV and PDF exports.
